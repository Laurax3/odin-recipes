Creamy chili oil pasta

It might be the best thing I have ever eaten, it's very easy to make, doesn't take much time And it only requires one pot and one (BIG) pan. Me and my girlfriend both ate so much we couldn't move afterwards and we still wanted more. It is supremely creamy and you can taste the chili oil very well, while not being overwhelmingly spicy. Goes well with chicken.

You can use any type of pasta; I used spaghetti, but any type of noodle will do. I haven't yet tried adding eggs to it, but I'm sure they would pair well.

    Ingredients:
  • 500g pasta (or noodles)
  • Half cup heavy cream (You can add a splash more if you want it to be really creamy, recommended if adding cheese)
  • 100-250g cheese
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp chili oil
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 4 tbsp reserved water
    • Optional:
  • Sesame seeds
  • Scallions
    Steps:
  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook your noodles until about 1 minute before al dente. Reserve a few tablespoons of noodle water, then drain.
  2. In a BIG pan over medium heat, combine heavy cream, soy sauce, chili oil, and sugar. Bring to a light simmer.
  3. Add the noodles and a bit of reserved noodle water. Toss continuously until the sauce thickens slightly and clings to the noodles.
  4. Add cheese and mix it until it starts to melt.
  5. Serve the noodles in a bowl and optionally garnish with sesame seeds and scallions.
    Notes:
  • I find it crazy how many recipes expect cooking newbies to just know this, but when the water is boiling, add salt before you add pasta. Most professional chefs recommend salting it until the water tastes like the sea, however I find that to be a tad too much.
  • Cheese isn't mandatory but strongly recommended. You can switch mozzarella for any other cheese of your liking, however if it's a hard cheese (like my beloved pecorino romano), it's better to serve it freshly grated as a garnish instead of mixing into the sauce.
  • Again, BIG pan.
